Meet Dr. Ashley Robinson

Dr. Robinson is a Doctor of Public Health with an epidemiology focus, an IBCLC, a Certified Labor Doula, and a Certified Lactation Educator. She has seven years of private lactation practice experience and serves families in New York City and Connecticut, with virtual care available nationwide.

She is also a peer reviewer for lactation journals, which shapes how she reads the research and how she teaches it: myth-busting, evidence-based, and always kind.

The Boob Doctor Podcast

Welcome to The Boob Doctor, where science meets real life. Hosted by Dr. Ashley Robinson, IBCLC, this podcast dives into all things breastfeeding, postpartum, and parenthood with honesty, humor, and research-backed insight. From milk supply myths to sex, sleep, and mental health, every episode empowers you to feed your baby your way.

Public Health in 5

Public Health in 5 exists to close the gap between peer-reviewed research and everyday decision-making. Most people never read public health journals. This show translates the data that shapes their lives into five minutes of clear, honest, evidence-based content delivered without jargon or agenda. Hosted by Dr. Ashley Robinson, a Doctor of Public Health with a concentration in Epidemiology and a Board-Certified Lactation Consultant with seven years of clinical experience in New York City, each episode tackles one fact, myth or statistic from the world of public health and makes it useful.

The book

The Supportive Partner

The Expert Guide for Dads Supporting Breastfeeding

How to Be Confident, Helpful, and Connected from Birth to Weaning

The ultimate handbook for new dads and partners who want to support breastfeeding with confidence, empathy, and purpose.

Becoming a parent is overwhelming, and when your partner is breastfeeding, it can be hard to know where you fit in. This book is a clear, judgment-free guide to showing up in ways that actually help, from the delivery room to the middle-of-the-night feeds. Written by an experienced IBCLC, it gives you the practical tools, emotional insight, and straight talk to be the kind of partner your baby and your partner can count on.

Inside, you'll learn

  • What breastfeeding is really like in the first days, weeks, and months, and what to expect
  • What to say (and what not to say) when your partner is frustrated, in pain, or unsure
  • How to help with night feeds, pumping, and bottle prep, even if you're not nursing
  • When to call for professional help, and how to advocate for your partner's needs
  • How to bond with your baby and build a strong, equal partnership from the start

Perfect for

  • First-time dads and non-birthing partners
  • Couples preparing for birth
  • Baby showers and new dad gifts
  • Doulas, childbirth educators, and lactation consultants who want to include partners

Your presence matters. You can make a difference without needing to breastfeed yourself.
